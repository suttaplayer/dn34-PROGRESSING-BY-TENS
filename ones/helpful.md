>  "Sāriputta, if a monk should wish, 'May I often dwell in a dwelling of emptiness,' then he should consider: 'On the path by which I entered the village for alms, or in the place where I wandered for alms, or on the path by which I returned from the village for alms, was there in my mind any **desire, passion, aversion, delusion, or irritation for forms cognizable via the eye**?' >  "**Heedfulness, great king. >  "Monks, any desire-passion with regard to the eye is a defilement of the mind. >  "In the same way, in any monk whose mindfulness immersed in the body is undeveloped & unpursued, the eye pulls toward pleasing forms, while unpleasing forms are repellent. This, monks, is **restraint**." [SN_nblm.txt, 555]
