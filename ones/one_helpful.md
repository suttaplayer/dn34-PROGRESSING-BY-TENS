# One > Helpful > Heedfulness with regard to skillful qualities


## Definition

### Heedfulness
**Heedfulness (Appamada)** is a single quality that guards the mind and is reckoned as the foremost among all skillful qualities [AN_nblm.txt, 175, 176][KN_Iti_nblm.txt, 306][SN_nblm.txt, 502]. It involves being **attentive** and **resolute**, particularly in abstaining from unskillful actions and developing good conduct [AN_nblm.txt, 54][MN_nblm.txt, 393]. Heedfulness means not giving way to heedlessness, laziness, or intimacy with sensual delight, but rather cherishing it as one's highest wealth [KN_Dhp_nblm.txt, 302]. It implies being mindful, alert, centered, sensitive, clear, and calm, especially when observing skillful mental qualities [KN_Iti_nblm.txt, 312].

### Skillful qualities
**Skillful qualities** are those mental qualities and actions that lead to welfare and happiness, are blameless, and are praised by observant people [AN_nblm.txt, 2, 27]. They are rooted in **lack of greed, lack of aversion, and lack of delusion** [AN_nblm.txt, 32][DN_nblm.txt, 241][MN_nblm.txt, 353]. Good bodily, verbal, and mental conduct are examples of skillful qualities [AN_nblm.txt, 2, 7, 54, 189, 190][DN_nblm.txt, 241][MN_nblm.txt, 396, 397]. The Dhamma itself, as taught by the Blessed One, consists of admirable teachings that lead to self-awakening and unbinding, and these are skillful qualities [AN_nblm.txt, 138, 139, 159, 160][DN_nblm.txt, 207, 208]. The full development of skillful qualities is essential for the holy life [MN_nblm.txt, 345].

## Benefits

### Heedfulness
Cultivating heedfulness brings several benefits:
*   It **keeps both kinds of benefits secure**: benefits in this life and in lives to come [KN_Iti_nblm.txt, 306, 307][SN_nblm.txt, 502].
*   It ensures that one **won't fall away from the goal** of the contemplative state when there is more to be done [MN_nblm.txt, 393, 394].
*   It helps in the **abandoning of unskillful qualities** and the **development of skillful qualities** [AN_nblm.txt, 54, 55].
*   It leads to **mindfulness and alertness** [AN_nblm.txt, 114].
*   It is crucial for the **ending of the effluents** and the attainment of gnosis [AN_nblm.txt, 44][KN_Iti_nblm.txt, 309, 311][MN_nblm.txt, 351, 424][SN_nblm.txt, 598].
*   When a monk dwells heedfully, **joy, rapture, calm, pleasure, and a centered mind** arise, and phenomena become manifest [SN_nblm.txt, 622].
*   It helps in **guarding the mind** against passion, aversion, delusion, and intoxication [AN_nblm.txt, 55].

### Skillful qualities
Cultivating skillful qualities yields numerous positive outcomes:
*   One **doesn't fault oneself**, observant people praise one, one's good reputation spreads, and one dies unconfused, reappearing in a good destination or heavenly world after death [AN_nblm.txt, 2].
*   They lead to a **decline in unskillful mental qualities** and an **increase in skillful mental qualities** [AN_nblm.txt, 36].
*   Practitioners become **peaceful in their faculties and hearts**, leading to peaceful bodily, verbal, and mental actions [AN_nblm.txt, 5].
*   They lead to the **abandoning of passion, aversion, and delusion**, preventing one from doing anything unskillful or evil [AN_nblm.txt, 37, 38][MN_nblm.txt, 353].
*   Discernment is developed, and ignorance is abandoned [AN_nblm.txt, 3].
*   They help in **remaining uninjured and unimpaired**, being blameless, and generating much merit [AN_nblm.txt, 6].
*   The holy life lived with training, discernment, release, and mindfulness results in **gnosis** right here and now, or non-return [AN_nblm.txt, 63, 64, 65, 66][KN_Iti_nblm.txt, 311].
*   Developing these qualities leads to **purity of the mind**, calm, and joy [AN_nblm.txt, 33][DN_nblm.txt, 209, 210][MN_nblm.txt, 405].
*   The **ending of craving** and thus the ending of suffering and stress [KN_Dhp_nblm.txt, 305].
*   It contributes to the **purification of beings**, overcoming sorrow, lamentation, pain, and distress, leading to the attainment of the right method and the realization of unbinding [DN_nblm.txt, 222][AN_nblm.txt, 61].
*   It leads to **growth, increase, and abundance** in the Dhamma-Vinaya [MN_nblm.txt, 373, 390].

## Causation and Conditions

### Heedfulness
*   **Conditions for heedfulness:**
    *   **Shame and compunction** precede clear knowing, and clear knowing precedes shame and compunction. Both shame and compunction are bright qualities that guard the world [AN_nblm.txt, 1][KN_Iti_nblm.txt, 310][SN_nblm.txt, 184, 568].
    *   Developing mindfulness of death acutely for the sake of ending effluents leads to dwelling heedfully [AN_nblm.txt, 111, 112].
    *   When one's mind is **guarded with regard to effluents** and qualities accompanied by effluents, heedfulness is established, and the five faculties (conviction, persistence, mindfulness, concentration, discernment) are developed [SN_nblm.txt, 598].
*   **Heedfulness as a condition for:**
    *   It serves as the **root, convergence point, and foremost quality** for all skillful qualities [AN_nblm.txt, 175, 176][DN_nblm.txt, 268].
    *   When developed, it keeps both benefits (in this life and next) secure [KN_Iti_nblm.txt, 306].
    *   It leads to **mindfulness and alertness** [AN_nblm.txt, 114].
    *   It enables a monk to live **full of ease in the here-and-now** and be appropriately aroused for the ending of effluents [KN_Iti_nblm.txt, 309].
    *   It helps in **abandoning apathy**, being hard to correct, and evil friendship [SN_nblm.txt, 184].
    *   It is why monks in higher training are encouraged to make an effort for the attainment of the as-yet-unattained, reaching the as-yet-unreached, and realization of the as-yet-unrealized [MN_nblm.txt, 424].

### Skillful qualities
*   **Conditions for skillful qualities:**
    *   **Lack of greed, lack of aversion, and lack of delusion** are the roots of skillful qualities [AN_nblm.txt, 32][DN_nblm.txt, 241][MN_nblm.txt, 353].
    *   **Clear knowing** precedes the arrival of skillful qualities [KN_Iti_nblm.txt, 310][SN_nblm.txt, 568].
    *   **Associating with people of integrity**, hearing the True Dhamma, appropriate attention, and practicing the Dhamma in accordance with the Dhamma are factors for stream-entry, which are skillful [DN_nblm.txt, 244][SN_nblm.txt, 607].
    *   Living in a civilized land, associating with people of integrity, directing oneself rightly, and having done merit in the past are helpful conditions [DN_nblm.txt, 274].
    *   **Conviction** in the Teacher's message is most helpful for visiting a teacher, which then leads to hearing the Dhamma, remembering it, penetrating its meaning, pondering, desire, willingness, contemplation, exertion, and ultimately realizing the ultimate truth with the body and seeing it with discernment [MN_nblm.txt, 430, 454].
    *   **Tranquility (samatha) and insight (vipassanā)** have a share in clear knowing; tranquility develops the mind, abandoning passion, while insight develops discernment, abandoning ignorance [AN_nblm.txt, 3][DN_nblm.txt, 269][SN_nblm.txt, 565, 566].
*   **Skillful qualities as conditions for:**
    *   They lead to the **abandoning of unskillful mental qualities** and the **increase of skillful mental qualities** [AN_nblm.txt, 36].
    *   Good bodily, verbal, and mental conduct lead to **not faulting oneself, praise from observant people, good reputation, and a good destination** after death [AN_nblm.txt, 2].
    *   The abandoning of passion leads to **awareness-release**, and the abandoning of ignorance leads to **discernment-release** [AN_nblm.txt, 4][DN_nblm.txt, 239].
    *   Cultivating the training (virtue, mind, discernment) leads to the **abandoning of passion, aversion, and delusion** [AN_nblm.txt, 37].
    *   When right view is supported by virtue, learning, discussion, tranquility, and insight, it leads to awareness-release and discernment-release [AN_nblm.txt, 70, 71].
    *   Consummation in virtue, concentration, discernment, and release, along with knowledge and vision of release, enables a monk to practice for both their own benefit and that of others [AN_nblm.txt, 69].
    *   The Dhamma is taught for the **abandoning of the gross, mind-made, and formless appropriation of a self**, leading to joy, rapture, calm, mindfulness, alertness, and a pleasant abiding [DN_nblm.txt, 209, 210].
    *   Virtue, concentration, and discernment, when nurtured, lead to the mind being **rightly released from effluents** [DN_nblm.txt, 229, 233, 235, 236].
    *   Good conduct leads to the possibility of entering and remaining in **effluent-free awareness-release and discernment-release** [MN_nblm.txt, 398].

## Application Areas

### Heedfulness
Heedfulness should be exercised in various instances and activities:
*   **General Conduct**: Abandoning bodily, verbal, and mental misconduct, and developing good conduct in these areas [AN_nblm.txt, 54, 55].
*   **Views**: Abandoning wrong view and developing right view [AN_nblm.txt, 54].
*   **Mind-States**: Guarding the mind from becoming impassioned, averse, deluded, or intoxicated with regard to relevant themes [AN_nblm.txt, 55].
*   **Daily Activities**: Being mindful and alert when going forward, going back, standing, sitting, lying down, determining on an action, looking toward and away, bending and extending limbs, carrying robes and bowl, eating, drinking, chewing, tasting, urinating, defecating, walking, sleeping, waking up, talking, and remaining silent [AN_nblm.txt, 114][MN_nblm.txt, 380, 392, 394, 404, 455, 480].
*   **Mindfulness of Death**: Developing mindfulness of death acutely to end effluents [AN_nblm.txt, 111, 112, 113].
*   **Mindfulness of Breathing**: Pursuing mindfulness of breathing to penetrate release [AN_nblm.txt, 87, 89, 90][MN_nblm.txt, 419, 471, 474, 475][SN_nblm.txt, 564, 601].
*   **Sense Media**: Being heedful concerning the six contact-media, ensuring disagreeable ideas do not consume the mind [SN_nblm.txt, 552].
*   **Overall Practice**: Living ardently and resolutely for the ending of effluents [KN_Iti_nblm.txt, 309].

### Skillful qualities
Skillful qualities are cultivated through numerous practices and apply across various aspects of the holy life:
*   **Bodily, Verbal, & Mental Conduct**: Abstaining from taking life, stealing, sexual misconduct, lying, divisive speech, harsh speech, and idle chatter. Cultivating compassion, generosity, truthfulness, and speech that promotes concord [AN_nblm.txt, 2, 7, 189, 190][DN_nblm.txt, 241][MN_nblm.txt, 396, 397, 415, 416, 417]. Not being covetous, having right thoughts like renunciation, non-ill will, and harmlessness [MN_nblm.txt, 397][DN_nblm.txt, 241].
*   **Training (Threefold)**: **Heightened virtue, heightened mind (concentration), and heightened discernment** [AN_nblm.txt, 35, 37, 38, 39, 40, 63, 64, 65, 66, 94][DN_nblm.txt, 229, 233, 235, 236, 266][KN_Iti_nblm.txt, 311, 312].
    *   **Virtue**: Dwelling restrained by Pāṭimokkha rules, consummate in behavior, seeing danger in the slightest fault [AN_nblm.txt, 35, 40, 63, 91, 92, 94, 141, 177, 178, 187][DN_nblm.txt, 266, 292, 298][MN_nblm.txt, 407, 460, 462][SN_nblm.txt, 572, 573, 594]. Virtues appealing to the noble ones are untorn, unbroken, unspotted, unsplattered, liberating, praised by the observant, ungrasped at, and conducive to concentration [AN_nblm.txt, 197, 204][DN_nblm.txt, 232, 245][SN_nblm.txt, 606, 609, 611, 615, 617, 622].
    *   **Concentration (Jhāna)**: Secluded from sensuality and unskillful qualities, entering and remaining in the four jhānas (stages of meditative absorption) [AN_nblm.txt, 35, 42, 49, 58, 74, 84, 85, 136, 155, 164, 172][DN_nblm.txt, 212, 272][MN_nblm.txt, 381, 382, 383, 384, 405, 411, 418, 456, 475, 481][SN_nblm.txt, 593]. Developing concentration based on desire, persistence, intent, or investigation [AN_nblm.txt, 599, 600].
    *   **Discernment**: Understanding things as they are, especially arising and passing away, leading to the right ending of stress [AN_nblm.txt, 156, 162, 168][DN_nblm.txt, 299][MN_nblm.txt, 410, 448][SN_nblm.txt, 593]. It is gained through investigation, examination, showing skepticism where it merits, and confidence where it merits [AN_nblm.txt, 6].
*   **Mindfulness**: Developing the four establishings of mindfulness (body, feelings, mind, mental qualities) [AN_nblm.txt, 173, 174][DN_nblm.txt, 222, 226, 228, 230, 234, 242, 274][MN_nblm.txt, 359, 360, 361, 362, 363, 364, 365, 366, 403, 471, 472, 473, 481][SN_nblm.txt, 557, 578, 579, 583, 584, 585, 586, 587, 588, 589, 590, 593]. This also includes mindfulness of in-and-out breathing [KN_Ud_nblm.txt, 316][MN_nblm.txt, 419, 471, 474, 475][SN_nblm.txt, 601].
*   **Addressing Defilements**: Developing contemplation of unattractiveness to abandon lust, goodwill to abandon ill will, mindfulness of in-and-out breathing to cut off distractive thinking, and perception of inconstancy to uproot conceit [AN_nblm.txt, 169][KN_Ud_nblm.txt, 344].
*   **Community and Learning**: Associating with people of integrity, listening to the Dhamma, discussing it, retaining it, and applying the mind to gnosis [AN_nblm.txt, 9, 11, 12, 91, 92, 96, 97, 98, 99, 100, 105, 115, 116, 131, 137, 140, 141, 149, 152, 153, 177, 178, 179, 180][DN_nblm.txt, 244, 250, 251, 252, 253, 266, 272, 281, 282, 283, 284, 292, 298][KN_Khp_nblm.txt, 325][KN_StNp_nblm.txt, 327, 328][MN_nblm.txt, 348, 430, 431, 453, 454][SN_nblm.txt, 522, 578, 607].
*   **Generosity**: Being freely generous, openhanded, delighting in being magnanimous, and responsive to requests [AN_nblm.txt, 15, 16][AN_nblm.txt, 198, 205][KN_Dhp_nblm.txt, 305][KN_Iti_nblm.txt, 306, 321][KN_Khp_nblm.txt, 325][KN_StNp_nblm.txt, 327].
*   **Persistence**: Keeping persistence aroused for abandoning unskillful and taking on skillful qualities; being steadfast and solid in effort [AN_nblm.txt, 1, 9, 93, 142, 168][DN_nblm.txt, 277, 293][MN_nblm.txt, 410, 443, 448][SN_nblm.txt, 594, 596].
