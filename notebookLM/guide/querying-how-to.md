### Causal Relationships for "Sense of Shame"

The "sense of shame" (and often "compunction" alongside it) is presented as a **skillful mental quality** and a **bright quality that guards the world**. Its presence or absence is directly linked to various outcomes, particularly concerning ethical conduct and the path to liberation.

Here are the specific causal expressions found:

*   **Direct Causation (A leads to B)**
    *   **"These two bright qualities guard the world."**
        *   Shame & compunction **guard the world**. This signifies that the presence of these qualities actively maintains a societal order and prevents promiscuity.
    *   **"Clear knowing precedes the arrival of skillful qualities; shame & compunction follow after."**
        *   While clear knowing is the direct precursor, shame and compunction are presented as **consequences or subsequent occurrences** of skillful qualities, indicating a supportive relationship.
    *   **"Having a sense of shame & having a sense of compunction, one is heedful."**
        *   The possession of shame and compunction **leads to** or results in the quality of heedfulness.

*   **Conditions & Prerequisites (A enables B)**
    *   **"Shame & compunction... is the first cause, the first requisite condition that leads to the acquiring of the as-yet-unacquired discernment that is basic to the holy life, and to the increase, plenitude, development, & culmination of that which has already been acquired."**
        *   A strong sense of shame (and compunction) is a **necessary prerequisite or enabling condition** for the acquisition, increase, development, and culmination of discernment in the holy life.

*   **Consequences & Results (B from A)**
    *   A disciple of the noble ones **feels shame at** (the thought of engaging in) bodily, verbal, and mental misconduct. This describes an internal feeling that results from the presence of the quality itself, serving as an intrinsic deterrent to unskillful actions.
    *   When one has done a mental action that leads to self-affliction or affliction of others, one should **feel distressed, ashamed, & disgusted with it**. This indicates that feeling shame is a proper and beneficial **result** of recognizing unskillful actions.

*   **Negated Causation (Absence of A leads to absence of B / A prevents B)**
    *   **"When anyone feels no shame in telling a deliberate lie, there is no evil, I tell you, he will not do."**
        *   The **absence of shame** (specifically in lying) **leads to** a complete lack of restraint, implying that one will commit *all* evils. This highlights shame as a crucial **preventative factor** against wrongdoing.
    *   **"A person without ardency, without compunction, is incapable of self-awakening, incapable of unbinding, incapable of attaining the unexcelled security from bondage."**
        *   The **absence of compunction** (paired with ardency, implying the absence of both) **prevents** the attainment of self-awakening, unbinding, and unexcelled security from bondage. Conversely, the presence of these qualities **enables** such attainments.
