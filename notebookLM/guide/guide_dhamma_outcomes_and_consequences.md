This guide outlines the various types of ultimate outcomes, rewards, and drawbacks associated with actions and qualities as described in the sources. It serves as a comprehensive vocabulary and framework for analyzing the "why" and "effects from" sections of responses, ensuring that all significant positive and negative consequences are considered.

#### 1. Favorable Outcomes and Benefits

These are the desirable states, rewards, and ultimate positive impacts resulting from skillful actions and developed qualities.

*   **Happiness & Welfare**: Skillful qualities lead to **welfare and happiness** for oneself and others.
    *   **In this life**: One doesn't fault oneself, observant people praise one, a good reputation spreads, and one dies unconfused. Good bodily, verbal, and mental conduct bring these rewards. Heedfulness secures benefit in this life.
    *   **In lives to come (heavenly rebirth)**: Rebirth in a **good destination, a heavenly world**. Merit is helpful for one who becomes a deva or a human being. Developing generosity, a life in tune, and goodwill leads to reappearance in a **world of bliss unalloyed**.
*   **Unbinding (Nibbāna) & Release (Vimutti)**: The ultimate goal, characterized by freedom from suffering.
    *   **Awareness-release & Discernment-release**: From the **fading of passion is awareness-release; from the fading of ignorance is discernment-release**. Entering and remaining in these states signifies cutting craving, ripping off fetters, and putting an end to suffering and stress. They are effluent-free.
    *   **Unexcelled freedom/safety from bondage**: Achieved through heedfulness.
    *   **The cooled state (Sīta)**: Realized by reining in, exerting, gladdening, and watching over the mind, being intent on the exquisite, and delighting in unbinding.
    *   **No further becoming/birth**: Achieved when ignorance and craving are abandoned. Those at peace, with vices evaporated, undesiring, and untroubled, **cross over birth and aging**.
*   **Purity & Cleansing**: The removal of defilements and unskillful qualities.
    *   **Cleansing of mind/awareness**: A mind is cleansed through proper technique. Purity of virtue, mind, view, and release are factors for exertion toward purity.
    *   **Ending of effluents (āsava)**: The ultimate goal of the holy life.
    *   **Purging away of wrong view**: Right view purges away wrong view, leading to the culmination of skillful qualities.
*   **Development of Skillful Qualities**:
    *   **Increased discernment, concentration, virtue, etc.**.
    *   **Mind becomes pliant, malleable, luminous, not brittle**: When attention is periodically given to concentration, uplifted energy, and equanimity themes.
    *   **Firmly established in truth**: Truth leads to non-regression.
*   **Stability of the Dhamma**: Heedfulness, being easy to correct, and learned monks attentively teaching the Dhamma, lead to the **stability, non-confusion, & non-disappearance of the True Dhamma**.
*   **Ability to help others**: Practicing for the welfare of many people and helping the True Dhamma to remain. The practice of merit benefits countless beings.

#### 2. Unfavorable Outcomes and Drawbacks

These are the undesirable states, losses, and negative impacts resulting from unskillful actions and undeveloped qualities.

*   **Suffering & Stress**: The direct consequence of unskillful actions and mental states.
    *   **Harm & Pain**: Unskillful qualities lead to **harm and pain**.
    *   **Self-affliction, affliction of others**: Results from unskillful bodily, verbal, and mental actions.
    *   **Increase of suffering/stress**: Reveling in fabrications leads to further suffering and stress. Stress arises from acquisition, ignorance, and fabrication. Craving is the ensnarer that leads to suffering.
*   **Rebirth in Lower Realms**:
    *   **Hell, animal womb, hungry ghosts**: Results from actions like being unvirtuous, envious, stingy, or engaging in sexual misconduct and lying.
*   **Mental Defilements & Hindrances**: Qualities that overwhelm awareness and weaken discernment.
    *   **Passion, aversion, delusion**: These are **roots of what is unskillful**. They defile the mind.
    *   **Five hindrances**: Sensual desire, ill will, sloth & drowsiness, restlessness & anxiety, uncertainty are obstacles that overwhelm awareness and weaken discernment, making it impossible to realize a superior human state.
    *   **Stinginess**: Leads to being placed in hell. Five forms of stinginess (monastery, family, gains, status, Dhamma) must be abandoned for the holy life to be fulfilled.
    *   **Conceit**: "I-making" or "mine-making" conceit-obsession hinders awareness and discernment release.
    *   **Dishonest livelihood**: Prevents right livelihood.
    *   **Laziness**: Overly slack persistence leads to laziness.
*   **Lack of Progress / Decline**:
    *   **Unskillful qualities increase, skillful qualities decline**: When following a fruitless practice.
    *   **Bewilderment & unprotected**: When one falls back on external causes (e.g., supreme being's creation) as essential, there is no desire or effort to do what should or shouldn't be done.
    *   **Lack of self-awakening, unbinding**: A person without ardency or compunction is incapable of these attainments.
    *   **Decline of True Dhamma**: Monks who are infatuated with construction work, gain, status, or assembly are expected to decline.
*   **Other negative outcomes**:
    *   **Being falsely accused**: A result of lying.
    *   **Rivalry & revenge**: A result of sexual misconduct.
    *   **Weariness & disappointment**: Trying to achieve something impossible, like drawing pictures in space, leads to this.
    *   **Loss of merit/harm to self**: Preventing others from giving gifts creates obstruction to merit and undermines oneself.
