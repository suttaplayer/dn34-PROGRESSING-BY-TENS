the noble tenfold path is actually an interface spec
a practitioner must coble together a concrete practice using various implementations:
eg. wakefulness is an implementation of right livelihood 

what is needed is build one's own toolbox and know when to apply the appropriate tool for the situation at hand

the confusion arises because there are different types of concepts:
* factors (eg. right speech)
* mental states (eg. concentrated-mind, scattered-mind)
* mental qualities (eg. conviction and the rest of the 37 wings)
* qualities which are unmapped (eg. sense of shame & compunction, heedfulness, 3 roots of skillful qualities, welcoming, relishing, remaining-fastened-to, delight)
* masterful practices (eg. wakefulness)
* events spawned in real-time whilst peforming another activity (eg. reflection, mindfulness, exertion)
* conducts (eg bodily - killing)
* well developed states (eg dispassion over passion, shedding over accumulating, etc)
* methodical steps (finding admirable friendship, hearing the dhamma, conviction, etc)
* straightforward stages (purity-of-virtue, purity-of-mind, purity-of-view, purity-of-overcoming-doubt, etc)
* perceptions (eg unattractiveness, loathsomeness)
* feelings and other dependently co-arisen phenomena
* fetters, obsessions, biases
* that is how you should train yourselves...

* progressing by tens training framework
    * dhammas that are helpful: these are qualities that are conducive to the training
    * dhammas to be developed: these are qualities that are the training of heightened mind/concentration
    * dhammas to be comprehended: this is the area/footing of where the problem is. the purpose of training is to grow dispassionate with these areas
    * dhammas to be abandoned: this is what the problem is with respect to the area (to be comprehended). the purpose of the training is to clearly see the drawbacks of these problems and fade the allure of the resultant problems
    * dhammas that are on the side of decline: these are the obstructions for training
    * dhammas that are on the side of distinction: these are the supports for training
    * dhammas that are hard to penetrate: these are qualities that represent the success criteria (verification/validation) of ones training
    * dhammas that should be made to arise: these are qualities that are transformative and should be part of the reflection and investigation training activity
    * dhammas that should be directly known: these are qualities that are a real-time self-reflection as to whether the training is on track or whether a detour has been taken
    * dhammas that should realised: these aare qualities which represent a catagorical examination of whether the remains a task to do with respect to heedfulness
