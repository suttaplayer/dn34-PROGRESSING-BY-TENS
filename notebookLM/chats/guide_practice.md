#### 11. Practice of Investigation
Investigation in Dhamma is crucial for understanding and progress:
*   **Cross-Questioning Dhamma**: Monks should cross-question one another about discourses, dissecting them: "How is this? What is the meaning of this?" to make open what isn't open, make plain what isn't plain, and dispel doubt.
*   **Seeking Guidance from Learned Monks**: Approach learned monks at regular intervals and ask & question them, "How is this, venerable sirs? What is the meaning of this?" to clarify hidden or unclear points and dispel doubts.
*   **Thinking & Evaluating Dhamma**: Think about and evaluate the Dhamma as heard and remembered, contemplating it with the intellect.
*   **Mental Examination**: In one's awareness, direct thoughts to the Dhamma in detail, evaluate it, and mentally examine it. This leads to sensitivity to meaning, joy, rapture, calm, and concentration.
*   **Three Modes of Investigation**: Investigate in terms of properties, sense media, and dependent co-arising.
*   **Analysis of Qualities**: As a factor for awakening, this involves investigating, carefully attending to, and examining internal and external qualities with discernment.
*   **Discernment**: Scrutinize all Dhamma with discernment for the right ending of suffering & stress.
