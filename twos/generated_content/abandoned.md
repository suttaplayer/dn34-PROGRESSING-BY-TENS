## Definition

**Ignorance** is primarily defined as a **lack of knowledge** regarding the fundamental truths of existence: stress, the origination of stress, the cessation of stress, and the path leading to the cessation of stress [MN_nblm.txt, 301][SN_nblm.txt, 456][SN_nblm.txt, 528]. It is considered the **ultimate impurity** [KN_Dhp_nblm.txt, 229] and precedes the emergence of unskillful qualities, often followed by a lack of shame and compunction [KN_Iti_nblm.txt, 235]. Furthermore, ignorance is identified as one of the **seven obsessions** [AN_nblm.txt, 116][DN_nblm.txt, 214][MN_nblm.txt, 320] and one of the **three effluents**, alongside sensuality and becoming [AN_nblm.txt, 101][DN_nblm.txt, 203][KN_Iti_nblm.txt, 241][MN_nblm.txt, 289, 303]. It is described as a **great delusion** that keeps beings transmigrating [KN_StNp_nblm.txt, 261] and has the detrimental effects of making one blind, sightless, and ignorant, thereby hindering discernment, leading to trouble, and preventing unbinding [AN_nblm.txt, 27, 28].

**Craving for becoming** is one of the **three forms of craving**, which also include sensuality-craving and non-becoming-craving [DN_nblm.txt, 192][MN_nblm.txt, 308, 429]. It is specifically identified as the **craving that propels further existence** (becoming), characterized by passion and delight, constantly seeking satisfaction [MN_nblm.txt, 308, 429]. This desire is also a component of the "yoke of becoming" [AN_nblm.txt, 38][DN_nblm.txt, 207] and is referred to as the "guide to becoming" [AN_nblm.txt, 37]. It acts as a **fetter** that binds beings, causing them to wander and transmigrate for prolonged periods [KN_Iti_nblm.txt, 234][DN_nblm.txt, 214], and is also categorized as an effluent [AN_nblm.txt, 101][DN_nblm.txt, 203][KN_Iti_nblm.txt, 241][MN_nblm.txt, 289, 303]. It is also explicitly stated as a **cause for the origination of actions** [AN_nblm.txt, 10].

## Causation and Conditions

The sources detail several cause-and-effect relationships involving ignorance and craving for becoming:

*   **Ignorance as a cause:**
    *   Ignorance is the **requisite condition for fabrications** (intentional actions) [AN_nblm.txt, 155, 156][KN_Ud_nblm.txt, 277, 279, 280, 281][MN_nblm.txt, 299, 350, 351][SN_nblm.txt, 455, 456, 458, 461, 462, 464, 467, 468, 472, 479, 484, 485].
    *   It leads to the **origination of effluents** (sensuality, becoming, and ignorance itself) [AN_nblm.txt, 101][MN_nblm.txt, 301, 303].
    *   It **precedes the emergence of unskillful qualities** [KN_Iti_nblm.txt, 235].
    *   It is considered the **root of all bad destinations** in this world and the next [KN_Iti_nblm.txt, 236].
    *   When unabandoned, ignorance can lead to **agitation** [MN_nblm.txt, 425].
    *   It is a condition by which beings are **defiled** [SN_nblm.txt, 512, 522].
    *   Ultimately, ignorance is the condition from which **stress and suffering** come into play [KN_StNp_nblm.txt, 260, 261].
*   **Craving for becoming as a cause:**
    *   Craving for becoming is a **requisite condition for clinging or sustenance** [AN_nblm.txt, 156][DN_nblm.txt, 172, 180][KN_Ud_nblm.txt, 278, 281][MN_nblm.txt, 350, 352][SN_nblm.txt, 459, 461, 462, 480, 484, 520, 524, 580].
    *   It causes the **origination of nutriment** [MN_nblm.txt, 296] and the **origination of clinging** [MN_nblm.txt, 297].
    *   It is one of the **three causes for the origination of action** (kamma), along with greed and aversion [AN_nblm.txt, 10, 31, 32].
    *   The Blessed One states that all stress, when arising, has **desire (craving) as its root and cause** [SN_nblm.txt, 583].
    *   Craving for becoming is also referred to as the "guide to becoming" [AN_nblm.txt, 37].
    *   When present, it leads to the **future accumulation of the five clinging-aggregates** and an increase in bodily and mental disturbances, torments, and distress [MN_nblm.txt, 437, 438].

## Complications & Perplexities

Several factors complicate and perplex one from abandoning ignorance and craving for becoming:

*   **Failure to discern:** An inability to discern the allure, drawbacks, and escape from phenomena like sensuality, forms, and feelings prevents their comprehension and abandonment [MN_nblm.txt, 316, 317, 318, 333][SN_nblm.txt, 550, 551, 552, 553]. The uninstructed person fails to discern the luminous mind defiled by incoming defilements [AN_nblm.txt, 1].
*   **Lack of right discernment/knowledge:** Without proper discernment, passion, aversion, and delusion (including ignorance) remain unabandoned [AN_nblm.txt, 5]. Delusion itself makes one blind, sightless, and ignorant, leading to the cessation of discernment [AN_nblm.txt, 27, 28].
*   **Immersion in craving:** Those immersed in craving may experience agitation and vacillation [DN_nblm.txt, 171].
*   **Five Hindrances:** The five hindrances—sensual desire, ill will, sloth & drowsiness, restlessness & anxiety, and uncertainty—are defilements that overwhelm awareness and weaken discernment, making it impossible to realize higher states [AN_nblm.txt, 75][DN_nblm.txt, 227]. These hindrances are explicitly stated as the "food" for ignorance, perpetuating its existence [AN_nblm.txt, 140, 141].
*   **Not focusing on drawbacks:** If one consistently fails to focus on the inherent drawbacks of phenomena that offer sustenance for clinging, craving continues to develop and proliferate [SN_nblm.txt, 480].
*   **Clinging and ingrained views:** Speculations about past or future, or clinging to sensuality, rapture, or certain feelings, can prevent ultimate unbinding [MN_nblm.txt, 406]. Attachment, obsession, and the failure to focus on drawbacks lead to the future accumulation of clinging-aggregates and increased stress [MN_nblm.txt, 437, 438].
*   **Lack of diligent practice (development):** Even with the wish for release, the mind may not be released from effluents due to a lack of development, specifically of practices like the four establishings of mindfulness, the four right exertions, and other factors for awakening [SN_nblm.txt, 523].
*   **Unskillful thinking and actions:** Thoughts imbued with sensuality, ill will, or harmfulness lead to blindness, lack of vision, and knowledge, ultimately causing discernment to cease [KN_Iti_nblm.txt, 245]. Actions stemming from greed, aversion, and delusion are unskillful, blameworthy, and ripen in pain, leading to further rounds of action [AN_nblm.txt, 31, 32].

## How To Abandon

Abandoning ignorance and craving for becoming involves a comprehensive approach to mental and ethical development:

*   **Developing Tranquility (Samatha) and Insight (Vipassanā):** These two qualities are essential for clear knowing. Developing tranquility cultivates the mind and leads to the abandonment of passion. Developing insight cultivates discernment and leads to the abandonment of ignorance [AN_nblm.txt, 4][MN_nblm.txt, 441, 444][SN_nblm.txt, 584].
*   **Abandoning Desire-Passion and Attachment:** The subduing and abandoning of desire-passion for sensuality, forms, feelings, and the six sense bases is presented as the very escape from these phenomena [MN_nblm.txt, 316, 317, 318][SN_nblm.txt, 506, 508, 540, 541, 542, 543, 544, 545, 546, 547, 549]. The ultimate goal, the "unfabricated," is described as the **ending of passion, aversion, and delusion** [SN_nblm.txt, 583, 585].
*   **Focusing on Inconstancy, Stress, and Not-Self:**
    *   Seeing **all fabrications as inconstant, stressful, and not-self** leads to disenchantment with stress and is the direct path to purity [KN_Dhp_nblm.txt, 231].
    *   Regularly focusing on the **arising and passing away of the five clinging-aggregates** is crucial, as it obliterates lingering conceit and desire [SN_nblm.txt, 518, 519].
    *   Developing the **perception of inconstancy** is a key practice; when steeped in this perception, the mind shrinks from worldly gains and fame, leading to equanimity or loathing, and ultimately can uproot the conceit "I am," leading to unbinding [AN_nblm.txt, 117, 130][KN_Ud_nblm.txt, 284][SN_nblm.txt, 492, 493].
*   **Practicing the Noble Eightfold Path:** This path is foundational for the **cessation of ignorance, fabrications, consciousness, name-&-form, the six sense media, contact, feeling, craving, clinging, becoming, and birth** [MN_nblm.txt, 296, 297, 298, 299, 301, 303][SN_nblm.txt, 506, 508, 509, 510, 587, 589]. It involves developing right view, right resolve, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, and right concentration [MN_nblm.txt, 296, 297, 298, 299, 301, 303][SN_nblm.txt, 587, 589].
*   **Abandoning the Five Hindrances:** By abandoning the five hindrances (sensual desire, ill will, sloth & drowsiness, restlessness & anxiety, and uncertainty), the mind becomes pliable, malleable, luminous, and rightly concentrated for the ending of effluents [AN_nblm.txt, 70][DN_nblm.txt, 227]. This abandonment is achieved by developing the four establishings of mindfulness [AN_nblm.txt, 135].
*   **Specific Practices:**
    *   **Mindfulness of breathing and unattractiveness:** Focusing on the foulness of the body, loathsomeness in food, distaste for the world, and inconstancy, combined with well-established mindfulness of in-&-out breathing, helps abandon passion and ignorance, and removes annoying thoughts [KN_Iti_nblm.txt, 244][AN_nblm.txt, 59].
    *   **Cultivating skillful qualities:** One should generate desire, endeavor, and persistence to prevent the arising of unskillful qualities, abandon those that have arisen, and encourage the arising, maintenance, and development of skillful qualities [MN_nblm.txt, 397, 398, 399, 400][SN_nblm.txt, 608, 609].
    *   **Guarding sense faculties:** Practicing restraint over the sense faculties prevents evil, unskillful qualities like greed or distress from assailing the mind [MN_nblm.txt, 357, 410, 417][SN_nblm.txt, 571].

## Areas Of Application

Ignorance and craving for becoming manifest across various aspects of experience and practice:

*   **Mind and Consciousness:** The mind is inherently luminous but becomes defiled by incoming defilements, a reality often not discerned by the uninstructed individual [AN_nblm.txt, 1]. Delusion specifically obstructs the mind, impairing discernment and leading to mental stress and sorrow [AN_nblm.txt, 27]. Craving, particularly for becoming, is intricately linked to the **mind-made body** [DN_nblm.txt, 174, 177].
*   **Six Sense Media:** Ignorance is fundamentally defined by a lack of insight into the origination, passing away, allure, drawbacks, and escape from forms, feelings, perceptions, fabrications, and consciousness, which are all experienced through the six sense media (eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, intellect) [SN_nblm.txt, 528, 529, 530]. The exhaustive nature of craving is highlighted by 108 types of craving-verbalizations, dependent on internal and external phenomena accessible through these senses [AN_nblm.txt, 62, 63, 64]. Desire-passion related to these six sense bases and their corresponding objects (forms, sounds, etc.) are recognized as defilements of the mind [SN_nblm.txt, 540, 541, 542, 543, 544, 545, 546, 547].
*   **Feelings:** Ignorance obsession specifically takes hold with **neither-pleasant-nor-painful feelings** if one fails to discern their origination, passing away, allure, drawback, or escape [MN_nblm.txt, 368, 433, 434]. Passion obsession arises with pleasant feelings, and resistance obsession with painful feelings [MN_nblm.txt, 368, 433, 434].
*   **Becoming (Existence):** Craving is the driving force for **further becoming** or existence [MN_nblm.txt, 308, 429]. The "yoke of becoming" ensnares individuals who fail to discern the nature of becoming (origination, passing away, allure, drawbacks, and escape), leading to an obsession with becoming-passion [AN_nblm.txt, 38].
*   **Actions (Kamma):** Greed, aversion, and delusion are identified as the **causes for the origination of actions** that are inherently unskillful, blameworthy, lead to painful consequences, and perpetuate further action [AN_nblm.txt, 10, 31, 32]. Ignorance drives fabrications (intentional actions), which in turn condition future consciousness and rebirth [SN_nblm.txt, 479].
*   **Clinging-Aggregates:** Infatuation, attachment, and confusion with respect to the five clinging-aggregates (form, feeling, perception, fabrications, consciousness) directly lead to their future accumulation and an increase in suffering and stress [MN_nblm.txt, 437, 438].
*   **Discussion and Debate:** When an individual lacks discernment about what is skillful or unskillful, they may resort to evasive or "eel-wriggling" responses in discussions due to fear of clinging or interrogation [DN_nblm.txt, 167, 168].
*   **States of "Being":** The "I am" conceit, along with "I am" desire and obsession, can persist even after the abandonment of lower fetters [SN_nblm.txt, 518, 519]. The very notion of "I am" is described as a **wavering and an objectification**, which are likened to a disease, a cancer, and an arrow [SN_nblm.txt, 579].
